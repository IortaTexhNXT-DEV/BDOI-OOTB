This Software Subscription Agreement (the **Subscription Agreement**) sets the terms of a subscription to iNXT BrokerVerse OOTB. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the MSA. Text in [square brackets] is a placeholder or an option.

# Subscription

## Right of use

1. During the Subscription Term, iorta TechNXT grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right to access and use the Platform for the internal business of the Client as a licensed insurance broker, by up to the Billable Users.
2. The Subscription includes: the right of use of the OOTB version; updates, releases and regulatory form updates released for all customers; and standard support under the Service Level Agreement.
3. Hosting is not included in the Subscription. The Platform is hosted either by iorta TechNXT under the Hosting and Infrastructure Services Agreement or by the Client, as stated in the Order Form.
4. The restrictions in the Perpetual Software Licence Agreement (Restrictions clause) apply to the Subscription with the necessary changes.

## Users and slabs

The Monthly Fee is calculated on graduated slabs: users 1 to 25 at the Small rate, users 26 to 100 at the Medium rate, users 101 to 300 at the Large rate and users above 300 at the Enterprise rate.

| Users in slab | Price per user per month, Year 1 (PHP) | Minimum billable users of the size |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 25 | 3,200.00 | Small: 10 |
| 26 to 100 | 2,800.00 | Medium: 26 |
| 101 to 300 | 2,400.00 | Large: 101 |
| Above 300 | 2,000.00 | Enterprise: 301 |

1. The size is set by the number of Subscribed Users. The minimum billable users of the size apply even if fewer users are active.
2. Example: 40 Subscribed Users are billed as 25 users at PHP 3,200.00 and 15 users at PHP 2,800.00, which is PHP 122,000.00 a month in Year 1.
3. The rates above are list rates of the price book dated 03 October 2026. The rates of the signed Order Form prevail.

## Adding and reducing users

1. The Client may add Subscribed Users at any time by written request or Order Form. Added users are billed from the first day of the month after they are activated, at the slab rates then applicable, for the rest of the Subscription Term.
2. The Client may reduce Subscribed Users only with effect from the start of a Renewal Term, on at least 60 days' written notice, and not below the minimum billable users of the size.
3. iorta TechNXT may check the number of active Named Users from the User Management records of the Platform. Active users above the Subscribed Users are billed from the month they first exceeded it.

# Term and renewal

## Initial Term

The Subscription starts on Go-Live and continues for the Initial Term stated in the Order Form ([12 / 36 / 60] months). The minimum Initial Term is 12 months. Use of the Platform before Go-Live for configuration, testing and training is covered by the Onboarding Fee.

## Auto-renewal

At the end of the Initial Term and of each Renewal Term, the Subscription renews automatically for a Renewal Term, unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the current term.

## Committed term

The Client may not terminate the Subscription for convenience during the Initial Term, unless the Order Form provides an early termination right. [If the Order Form provides one, the Client pays an early termination fee equal to [all / 50% of] the Monthly Fees for the remainder of the Initial Term.]

# Fees and billing

## Onboarding Fee

The Onboarding Fee is invoiced 40% on signing of the Order Form, 40% on UAT sign-off and 20% on Go-Live, as stated in the Implementation SOW.

## Monthly Fee

1. The Monthly Fee is invoiced monthly in advance, on the first day of each month, from Go-Live. The first and last months are prorated by day.
2. The Monthly Fee increases by 5% at each anniversary of Go-Live, for the Initial Term and every Renewal Term.
3. [Option: if the Order Form states that the Client prepays the Subscription yearly in advance, the Monthly Fee for the year is invoiced at the start of the year with the prepayment discount in the Order Form.]
4. Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Fees exclude VAT, which is added at the rate in force. Withholding tax is handled as stated in the Taxes clause of the MSA.

## Suspension for non-payment

1. If an undisputed invoice remains unpaid 60 days after its due date, iorta TechNXT may suspend access to the Platform after giving the Client at least 10 Business Days' written notice that states the amount due and the suspension date.
2. During suspension, Client Data is kept intact and the Client's System Administrator may still sign in to export reports, unless the Client has also breached the Acceptable use clause.
3. iorta TechNXT restores access within 1 Business Day after receiving payment of all overdue amounts. Fees continue to accrue during suspension.
4. If an overdue amount remains unpaid 30 days after suspension starts, iorta TechNXT may terminate the Subscription under the MSA.

# Acceptable use

The Client shall not, and shall ensure its Users do not:

1. use the Platform in breach of law, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012, the Insurance Code and the tax laws;
2. upload malicious code, or content that infringes the rights of others;
3. attempt to gain unauthorised access to the Platform or to other customers' data, test its security without written consent, or interfere with its operation;
4. share sign-in credentials, or let a person use the Platform under another person's account;
5. use automated means to extract data in volumes that degrade the service, except through the delivered exports and reports.

iorta TechNXT may suspend a User account that breaches this clause, informing the Client immediately.

# Service levels

Support, severity levels, response and restore targets, service credits, escalation and reporting are as stated in the Annual Maintenance and Support Agreement and Service Level Agreement, which applies to the Subscription without a separate AMC fee. Availability targets apply when iorta TechNXT hosts the Platform, under the Hosting and Infrastructure Services Agreement.

# Client Data on exit

## Data during the term

The Client owns its Client Data. During the Subscription Term, the Client can export Client Data at any time through the delivered reports and exports (XLSX, CSV and PDF).

## Data return

1. On expiry or termination, the Client may request, within 60 days, a full export of the Client Data. iorta TechNXT delivers it within 30 days of the request as: (a) a PostgreSQL database dump of the Client's database; (b) an archive of the uploaded documents in their original formats; and (c) CSV extracts of the main registers listed in the Documentation, with the data dictionary.
2. One full export is included. Further exports, data mapping to a new system and transition assistance are provided at the day rates of the Order Form.
3. During the 60-day period, iorta TechNXT keeps the Client Data and may keep read-only access open for the System Administrator at [no charge / the Monthly Fee prorated].

## Data deletion

1. After the export is delivered and confirmed by the Client, or 60 days after expiry or termination if the Client makes no request, iorta TechNXT deletes the Client Data from the production and non-production environments within 30 days.
2. Backups that contain Client Data expire under the backup retention in the Hosting and Infrastructure Services Agreement (at most 35 days for daily backups, 12 months for monthly copies, and 10 years for year-end copies only if the Client has instructed that retention), and are not restored except to comply with law.
3. iorta TechNXT gives the Client a written certificate of deletion within 10 Business Days of completing deletion, signed by its data protection officer.
4. iorta TechNXT may keep Client Data longer only where law requires, under the Data Processing Agreement, and keeps it confidential and protected.

# Term and termination

1. This Subscription Agreement lasts for the Subscription Term.
2. Either Party may terminate it for cause under the MSA.
3. On expiry or termination: the right of use ends; the Client pays all Fees due up to the end date; the Client Data on exit clause applies.
