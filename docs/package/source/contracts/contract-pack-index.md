# About this document

This document is the entry point to the iNXT BrokerVerse OOTB contract pack of iorta TechNXT Corp. (**iorta TechNXT**). It has two parts:

- **Part 1, for the client:** a cover letter that goes with the contract set sent to [Client legal name] (the **Client**) for signature.
- **Part 2, internal to iorta TechNXT:** the index of the documents, which documents apply to each commercial model and hosting option, the signing order, the order of precedence, and the checklists for the sales and legal teams.

> Part 2 is internal. Send the Client the cover letter and the documents that apply, not this whole document. The floor prices and approval limits stay in the Negotiation Playbook, which is never sent.

All documents in the pack are templates for discussion, subject to review by the parties' legal counsel. Text in [square brackets] is a placeholder or an option.

## Order of precedence

The order of precedence is set in the MSA (Order of precedence clause). If documents conflict, the first in this list prevails:

1. The Data Processing Agreement, on matters of personal data.
2. The Order Form, only for that purchase and only where it expressly states that it overrides the MSA.
3. The Master Services Agreement.
4. The schedules named in the Order Form, with their annexes: the Perpetual Software Licence Agreement or the Software Subscription Agreement, the Change Request Procedure, the Annual Maintenance and Support Agreement and SLA, the Hosting and Infrastructure Services Agreement, the Service Catalogue and Rate Annex, the Customer Responsibilities and RACI Annex and the Exit and Transition Plan.
5. The Implementation SOW.
6. The Documentation.

Specific rules in the documents themselves:

| Rule | Where it is stated |
|---|---|
| Rates in the Order Form prevail over the Service Catalogue and Rate Annex | MSA; Rate Annex |
| The clauses of the MSA, the Subscription Agreement, the Hosting Agreement and the DPA prevail over the Exit and Transition Plan | Exit and Transition Plan |
| The clauses of the MSA, the SOW and the Support Agreement prevail over the RACI Annex | RACI Annex |
| Between iorta TechNXT and the Client, the Licence Agreement prevails on the scope of the licence; the Escrow Agreement prevails on the deposit, verification and release procedure | Source Code Escrow Agreement |
| Acceptance certificates and Change Request forms record facts and approvals and do not amend the MSA, the Order Form or the SOW; an approved Change Request changes the plan and fees of the Order Form from its approval date | MSA; Change Request Procedure; certificates |
| The MSA and the Order Forms replace earlier proposals and understandings, including the Letter of Award, on their subject | MSA, General (entire agreement) |

> Recommended for legal review: the MSA does not rank the schedules among themselves. The recommended rule is that the schedule that deals specifically with the subject prevails (the Hosting Agreement on hosting, the Support Agreement on support, the licence or subscription schedule on the right of use). Legal counsel to confirm and, if agreed, add it to the MSA.
