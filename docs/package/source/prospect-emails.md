# How to use these templates

## Purpose

These are the e-mails the iorta TechNXT sales team sends to a Philippine non-life insurance broker, from the first introduction to the welcome after signing. Each template gives its purpose, when to send it, subject line options, the body with placeholders, and the attachments. A plain-text copy for pasting into an e-mail client is kept next to this document (`Prospect_Email_Templates.txt`).

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Fill with |
|---|---|
| [Mr./Ms.] [Surname] | The addressee as they sign their own e-mails. Use the first name only once they do. |
| [Company] | The broker's registered or trade name |
| [Your name], [Title], [Mobile], [E-mail] | The sender's details |
| [Date], [Time] | Written as 03 October 2026, 10:00 AM PHT |
| [Pain point] | The issue the prospect raised, in their own words |
| [Number] users, [Number] lines | Figures from the discovery or the workshop |
| [Link] | Calendar, meeting or registration link |

## Rules for every e-mail

- One request per e-mail, stated in the last paragraph, with a date.
- Personalise the first two lines. A template sent unchanged reads like a template.
- Keep the body under 150 words, except the proposal, contract and welcome e-mails.
- Use the system's names for features (Placement Slip, Month-End Close, BIR Form 2307). Do not use superlatives.
- Prices only from the pricing workbook or the proposal, always "exclusive of 12% VAT".
- Attach PDF, never editable files, except the pricing calculator when the prospect asks for it.
- Recommendation: send between 9:00 and 11:00 AM PHT, Tuesday to Thursday, and avoid the days before a long weekend or a holiday.
