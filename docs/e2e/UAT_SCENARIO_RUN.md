## Defects found and fixed

The scenario runs the cycle with business dates spread over six months, so it exposed places where a document keyed in
late was booked on the day it was keyed in, or where one step did not carry its data to the next. Each fix is covered by
`backend/test/uat-disconnects.test.js`.

| Area | What was wrong | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting dates | Policies, direct-bill commission and endorsements were journalised on the keying date | Journals take the document's issue date; credit days run from the issue or later inception |
| Quotation product | A Householder quotation from a broker slip became Fire (non-package) and asked for a placement slip; CTPL became Motor | The product type wins over the line of business (migration 0175) |
| CTPL tariff | A quotation discount reduced the fixed CTPL premium | The discount applies to the other covers only |
| Bank accounts | A receipt deposited to a reconciled bank account debited a different GL cash account | The bank account's linked GL account is used |
| Renewals | A renewal completed in the renewal workspace had no bill or producer commission; fire renewals priced from zero | Full policy term with bill and commission; renewal quotations priced on the expiring net premium |
| Collections | Remittance ageing dated a collection by its keying date | Collection date stored and used (migration 0176) |
| Settlements | A settlement voucher could include premium collected after the settlement period | Only collections up to the period end; the cheque pays the voucher's payables |
| Registers and dashboards | Production register and executive trend used the record creation date | Business (issue) dates |
| Claims | A claim settled with a past date was journalised on the keying date | Settlement date used for the claim and its journal |
| Month end | Policies migrated at go-live were reported as policies without accounting | Migrated in-force policies excluded from that check |
