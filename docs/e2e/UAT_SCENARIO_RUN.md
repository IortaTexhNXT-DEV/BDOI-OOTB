## Defects found and fixed

Release verification of 04 October 2026 on the merged release (every package on `brokerverse-platform`), API started with
the production settings of the go-live document on a fresh database. The first run failed 3 of 373 steps; the fixes:

| # | Step | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Billing and collection: cancellation for non-payment of POL-2026-00027 (a CTPL-only policy) | 409 "has no net premium to compute a return on": the computed cancellation return refused every policy with a net premium of 0; a CTPL-only policy carries the IC tariff as its gross premium, taxes included | Product (BV-DEF-009): the return of a tariff-only policy is computed on the tariff amount with no premium tax of its own (`cancellations/service.js`, test in `ops-accounting.test.js`) |
| 2 | Money: commission of the dealer F&I officer (sub-agent) | 409: no licence on the licence register (the IC licence check of the compliance package blocks the payout) | Scenario set-up: a compliance officer persona (imelda.navarro) records the licences of both referrers on Compliance > Insurance Commission > Licence Register |
| 3 | Reports: Dealer Production returned no rows | The distribution package's report counts the business of distribution channels; the scenario had none | Scenario set-up: the car dealer tie-up as a dealer group and branch linked to the F&I officer; the dealer-sourced motor prospects carry the branch |
| 4 | Set-up: compliance officer persona | 400: designation "Compliance Officer" not in the Designation master | Product (BV-DEF-010): department CMP and designation DSG-CPO delivered in the masters seed (test in `masters.test.js`) |

Added for the release verification: the phase "Compliance: customer due diligence" (government ID of every retail
client, SEC registration, signatory and beneficial owners of every corporate client, one PEP-owned client rated High
with its EDD review prepared by Operations and approved by the compliance officer) and the package G steps (sales
activities on the prospects of the month, motor quotations priced on the covers chosen in the quote wizard, supplier
invoice with EWT approved by the Accounting Manager and paid with its BIR 2307, fixed asset registered and sold).
